A title logo for an animated family comedy series, made of only two lowercase letters: "if".
Exactly two letters, the word "if", nothing else, no other text.

The letters are big, chunky, playful 3D lettering, slightly irregular and hand-made, with a warm,
friendly cartoon personality, in the stylized 3D animated look of @1_style_plan (soft pastel palette, warm
light, rich sculpted shapes).

Each letter is BUILT from a joyful pile of human inventions through the ages, assembled like a sculpture,
in rough chronological order from bottom to top: flint stones, a stone wheel, a clay pot, a bronze spear
head, a scroll, a compass, a quill pen, an hourglass, a printing press letter block, a telescope, a pocket
watch, gears and cogs, a light bulb, a telephone, a gramophone horn, a camera, a small rocket, a lightbulb
idea spark. The dot of the "i" is a small glowing flip-top lighter flame (no brand logo).

Clear readable silhouette of "if" at first glance; the inventions are the texture and the detail.

Background: plain flat neutral light grey (#E6E6E6), no shadow on the background, no floor, isolated
object, centred, generous empty margin around it, so it can be cut out easily.

Avoid: any other letters or words, a third letter, misspelling, capital letters, brand logos, characters,
people, busy background, gradient background, watermark.
