# "BOR" style — the bible of the director's line

*French original: [STYLE_BIBLE_FR.md](STYLE_BIBLE_FR.md)*

Extracted from 42 of Jérémie's drawings, analysed on 2026-10-07. Six key references are attached to every
generation. Some of them are his own drawings passed through an AI upscaler (retouched part by part): the style
is his, and these serve as **render targets** (his caricature, in volume).

## 1. Proportions: caricature
- **Mass at the top, support at the bottom**: huge chest, shoulders and traps; shorter pelvis and legs. The head
  is **small relative to the torso** on heavy characters, **big** on thin ones and children.
- **Oversized hands and forearms**, at least 1.5× realistic. Big feet too.
- **Hunched posture**: the head sits in front of the shoulders, the back humps, the character "weighs".

## 2. Shapes: round AND angular
- **Masses are round**: balloon muscles, bellies, noses, lips.
- **Joints and planes are angular**: square jaws, cheekbones, elbows, knuckles, brows.
- The contrast creates the rhythm. Never all round (glossy 3D), never realistic.

## 3. Faces: expressive, never neutral
- **Very strong nose** (long, bulbous or hooked), **thick lips**, **heavy brows**.
- Eyes: either **small and deep-set** under heavy lids, or **round and bulging** (often with glasses).
- **Asymmetry**: one brow higher, a crooked mouth, grimaces.
- Wrinkles, eye bags, stubble, strands of hair falling over the face.

## 4. Hands: the signature
- **Highly stylised**: wide fingers **segmented in blocks** (square phalanges), prominent knuckles.
- Often **spread like claws** or **clenched into big fists**, sometimes foreshortened towards the viewer.
- Bandages and wrapped wrists are frequent.

## 5. Pose and movement
- **Dynamic poses, low centre of gravity**: crouching, legs apart, weight forward.
- **Foreshortening**: a fist or hand coming towards the camera.
- A silhouette readable at first glance.

## 6. Line and rendering
- **Confident line, variable weight**, pencil or ink.
- **Hatching and cross-hatching** for volumes (muscles, skin, shadows), tattoo patterns.
- Colour: **saturated flats + soft shading**, Franco-Belgian comic style; warm reds and oranges.
- Signature: "BOR".

## Style prompt block (pasted into every generation)
```
Drawn in the personal cartoon style of @style_refs: strong caricature, exaggerated proportions. Huge upper
body and shoulders, heavy hunched posture with the head pushed forward, short legs. OVERSIZED, very stylized
hands with blocky segmented fingers and big knuckles, oversized feet. Big round masses (muscles, nose, lips)
contrasted with angular planes (square jaw, cheekbones, brows, elbows, knuckles). Very expressive asymmetric
face: big nose, thick lips, heavy brows, heavy-lidded deep-set eyes or wide bulging eyes. Dynamic pose with a
low centre of gravity. Confident ink lines with varying weight, visible hatching on the volumes.
NOT smooth Pixar 3D, NOT realistic anatomy, NOT a neutral face.
```
