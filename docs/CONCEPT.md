# if — Concept and format

*French version: [CONCEPT_FR.md](CONCEPT_FR.md)*

## The idea
A person from one era (Zak the caveman first, then his descendants) faces a simple problem. An object from
another era would solve it instantly, but he has no idea how to use it. Voice-over at the opening and the end,
title **"if"**.

## Format
- **One fixed shot**: character in the foreground, one architectural element, a living background.
- Preceded and followed by a **vertical camera move** sky ↔ scene.
- **60 to 90 seconds.**
- Audience: families, children 6–12. Soft finish: clean thin line, hatching as light accents, warm colours,
  Zak grumpy but endearing.

## Style
Stylised 3D that keeps the director's own line (inking, hatching, slightly stepped animation, in the spirit of
recent stylised animated features). **Not glossy "studio 3D", not realistic.** See [STYLE_BIBLE.md](STYLE_BIBLE.md).

## The recurring device: the hand
An elastic arm from off-screen, in a teacher's tweed sleeve. It is what the audience wishes it could do:
*"For heaven's sake, here's a lighter!"* It comes back in every episode.

## The lighter gag — choreography of THE HAND (episode 1)
Fixed shot, same point of view. The hand always comes from off-screen.

1. **STOP** — the hand bursts up from the bottom of the frame, foreground, centre, palm towards Zak, who stops striking.
2. **Magic trick** — it spins like a conjurer and produces a **chrome-capped lighter**, presented in the foreground,
   centre, close to camera, with a "divine / commercial" effect (halo, sparkle, a small *ting*). With one hand it
   flips the cap with the thumb, lights it (small clean flame) and snaps it shut, fast and masterful. It is the
   only flame in the film: proof that the object works… before Zak misuses it. The background goes out of focus,
   like an ad.
3. It drops back out at the bottom. Focus returns to the normal shot **before** the hand reappears from the right,
   at Zak's height.
4. **Double finger snap** to catch Zak's attention. Zak is **suspicious** (side glance, raised brow, gripping his flints).
5. **New appearance** (same magic gesture): it **hands him the lighter**.
6. Zak **jumps** and **freezes in a startled pose** (comic beat).
7. He **takes the object**; the hand vanishes off-screen.

→ Then he stares at the lighter… and strikes it against the flint, just like before.
